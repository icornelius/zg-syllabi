# Presentation report

Write a report on the syllabus item on which you presented.
This is due one week after your presentation.
It should be between 500 and 750 words.

If your presentation was done as a pair, the report may be co-authored.
Both authors will receive the same grade.
If you presented as a pair but write separately, overlap should be minimal.


# Midterm project

Choose from the following options:

1. Discuss a disputed question in the history of the English language.
Use one of the course readings as your launching point; engage with at least two other scholarly sources (on our syllabus or not).
1. Write a report on one of the corpora discussed in @KytoHistoricalCorporaEnglish2025.
Briefly describe the corpus (scope, aims, sources) and the search options.
Run some searches and discuss the results.
1. Create an undergraduate course module on *Oxford English Dictionary*, including lesson plans and a written assignment.
Write a model response to your assignment.
1. Do something else. Clear your idea with me in advance.

This should be between about 750 and 1000 words.

# Final project

Choose from the following options:

1. Write an argued research essay on a disputed question in the history of the English language.
1. Compare the language of an item in @BurnleyHistoryEnglishLanguage2000 with the language of another item not printed there.
Your second item should be sourced from a scholarly edition or from special collections of the Newberry Library or Loyola University Libraries, or digital images of an archival document or early printed book held by another library.
Justify your selections.
Identify both items, then discuss features of orthography, phonology, morphology, syntax, lexis, and discourse (genre, form, socio-cultural function).
Unless your second item is available as a web resource, supply a transcription or digital images as supporting materials.
1. Read another recent textbook on the history of the English language and compare it with @BarberEnglishLanguageHistorical2025, and other materials used in this course.
Which textbook would you prefer in an undergraduate course, and why?
Could the history of the English language be taught at high-school level?
1. Do something else. Clear your idea with me in advance.

This should be about 4000 words.

Students who seek to fulfill the MA program's pre-1800 requirement with this course must write their final project on pre-1800 materials.
Similarly, students who seek to fulfill the PhD program's Medieval and Renaissance Requirement with this course must write their final project on medieval or early modern materials.
