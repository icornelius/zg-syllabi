- Describe the language of documents written in earlier stages of English --- orthography, phonology, morphology, syntax, lexis, and discourse --- in standard linguistic terminology, and relate the features observed to the date, provenance, and purpose of the document.
- Employ the principal tools of research in the history of English --- historical dictionaries, corpora, scholarly editions, and archival and early printed sources --- to frame and sustain an argument about linguistic change in an extended scholarly essay.
