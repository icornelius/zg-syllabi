\textbf{Claim} \scriptsize{(\emph{CR} 6)} &
Precise, contestable, and consequential; makes plain what follows if readers accept it, and why that matters &
Precise and contestable; the essay's purpose is clear from the outset &
Over-general, or so narrowly factual that little is at stake; shifts between opening and close &
Absent, or a statement no informed reader would dispute \\
\hline

\textbf{Argument} \scriptsize{(\emph{CR} 5, 7--9, 11.3)} &
Reasons ordered by a visible logic and warranted where a reader would balk; acknowledges the strongest objection and answers it &
Reasons support the claim and are signposted; objections are noted, if not fully answered &
Unexplained leaps from reason to claim; alternative views ignored or dismissed rather than engaged &
Assertion in place of reasoning; the line of thought cannot be reconstructed \\
\hline

\textbf{Organization} \scriptsize{(\emph{CR} 10.2--3, 11.4)} &
Each section's function is evident at its outset; order motivated by the argument rather than by the sources or the chronology &
Sections follow a logical order and are announced to the reader &
Repetitive, or a source-by-source `patchwork'; paragraphs could be reordered without loss &
No discernible plan \\
\hline

\textbf{Introduction and Concl.} \scriptsize{(\emph{CR} 14)} &
Opens by posing a problem readers have reason to care about; closes by stating what the argument has settled and what it opens &
Introduction states the problem and the claim; conclusion does more than restate them &
Perfunctory or clichéd; promises the essay does not keep &
Absent, or inconsistent with the essay body \\
\hline

\textbf{Evidence} \scriptsize{(\emph{CR} 7.5, 12.5--6)} &
Sufficient, representative, and authoritative; quoted or reported accurately and at the right scale; every item interpreted, not left to speak for itself &
Relevant, accurate, and usually explained &
Thin or one-sided; quotations dropped in without comment; paraphrase drifts from its source &
Missing where the argument needs it, or misrepresents its source \\
\hline

\textbf{Citations} \scriptsize{(\emph{CR} 12.7--9)} &
Complete and correctly formatted; placed so readers can always tell whose words and ideas these are &
Present where needed and verifiable; minor errors of format &
Irregular in format; attribution deferred or bundled, so the extent of the borrowing is unclear &
Missing or unverifiable \\
\hline

\textbf{Style} \scriptsize{(\emph{CR} 15)} &
Subjects name the characters, verbs the actions; sentences end on their new information; emphasis falls where the argument needs it &
Clear at the level of the sentence; occasional abstraction or wordiness &
Habitual nominalization, evasive passives, or padding; readers must reread to parse &
Meaning frequently unrecoverable \\
\hline

\textbf{Grammar and Form} &
Consistent control of formal academic written English; clean and correct presentation throughout &
Occasional slips that do not impede a reader &
Patterned error, characteristic of haste or inattention &
Error dense enough to obstruct reading; format requirements disregarded \\
\hline
