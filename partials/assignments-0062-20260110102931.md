For conventions of academic writing consult [*The Chicago Manual of Style*](https://www.chicagomanualofstyle.org/home.html), 18th edition, and @BoothCraftResearch2024 (available on course reserve in Cudahy Library).
Use a Chicago citation style; include a complete, accurate, and correctly formatted list of works cited.
Prior to submission run the checks for clarity described in *The Craft of Research*, sections 15.2, 15.3, and 15.5.

Avoid verbal overlap between assignments.
If your prior work is important to a subsequent assignment, cite your prior work and summarize the key finding.

Print a paper copy (double-spaced, with page numbers) and upload a PDF to Sakai.
If you are unable to meet a deadline, request an extension in advance.
