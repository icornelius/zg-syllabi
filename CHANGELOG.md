# Changelog

This changelog documents substantive changes to the syllabi published from this repository.
The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), but the scope is limited to the syllabi themselves, ignoring the repository's own tools and documentation.
The intended reader is a student who wants to know what has changed in a syllabus since the version they downloaded.

Versions are designated with the format YEAR-MONTH.VERSION, where MONTH is the two-digit month in which the semester begins (usually 01 or 08) and VERSION is a counter that restarts at 1 each semester.
Releases before 2026-08 were designated with a dot in place of the hyphen, and their counter began at 0.
Where a release was rebuilt without a change to the text, the rebuild is designated with a further suffix, e.g. 2026.01.2-1.

Each semester opens with an initial release, recorded here as such.
There is no account of how a semester's syllabi differ from those of the semester before: each syllabus is a new document, addressed to a new class, and a student has no occasion to consult the previous version.
The entries that follow an initial release record changes made to that semester's syllabi while the semester was under way.

Changes are cited by the commit that made them.

## [2026-08.1] - 2026-08-16

Initial release for the Fall 2026 semester: "Chaucer" (ENGL 322) and "History of the English Language" (ENGL 406).

## [2026.01.2-1] - 2026-04-01

No change to the text.
The release build for 2026.01.2 failed on a mistyped workflow setting; this release attaches the PDF that release should have carried ([021a5be]).

## [2026.01.2] - 2026-04-01

### Changed

- ENGL 413, Schedule: move the proposal deadline from Friday 27 March to Thursday 2 April, and begin project presentations on 20 April ([619afeb])
- ENGL 413, Schedule: supply readings for three meetings previously listed as "TBD" or "other readings TBD" (16 March, 13 April, 15 April) ([619afeb])

### Fixed

- ENGL 413, Schedule: correct the page range cited for McKerrow, *Prolegomena*, chapter 3 ([619afeb])
- ENGL 413, Bibliography: re-export from Zotero 8 ([0d27b3c])

## [2026.01.1] - 2026-02-09

### Added

- ENGL 413: new section "License and rights", marking the syllabus [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and reminding students that most course readings are under copyright ([35334bb])
- ENGL 413, Schedule: add an exercise in enumerative bibliography, a Newberry Library research guide, and the Bodleian catalogue description and sample images of MS Hatton 115 ([ee32ab0])

### Fixed

- ENGL 413, Texts: give the steps for reaching the *Chicago Manual of Style* from off campus ([4282a1a])
- ENGL 413, Schedule: correct the rendering of an *OED* citation ([4282a1a])

## [2026.01.0] - 2026-01-10

Initial release for the Spring 2026 semester: "Textual Criticism" (ENGL 413).

## [2025.08.1] - 2025-09-21

### Added

- ENGL 320, Exams: state the grading criteria for passage identification, previously unpublished ([7213915])

### Changed

- ENGL 320, Schedule: give *Beowulf* a fourth meeting, and move *Judith* and the Alfred and Wulfstan readings back one meeting each; the review day before the first mid-term exam is dropped ([b770fbf])
- ENGL 320, Exams: revise the instructions for passage identification; a passage may be anonymous, the speaker should be identified where relevant, and the features open to comment now include exposition and description ([6d684ba])
- ENGL 299, Note collections: a note may be identified by timestamp as well as by number, and a point locator is required only where one is relevant ([bc77d67])

### Fixed

- ENGL 299, Schedule: correct a typo ([fd0afa0])

## [2025.08.0] - 2025-08-23
